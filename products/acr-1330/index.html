ACR-1330 AI Model-Driven Patrol Sweeper

The ACR-1330 is a commercial patrol-and-sweep robot built around an on-board end-to-end embodied AI model. It combines two capabilities in one machine: it patrols, and it sweeps only where debris is actually present — rather than running at full load along every metre of route. This substantially reduces energy consumption and brush wear. A 254 TOPS automotive-grade computing platform, IPX6 protection and a LiFePO₄ power system support a comprehensive cleaning efficiency of 3,000–4,500 m²/h at a 1330 mm working width, making it suitable for park roads, scenic areas, plazas and municipal footpaths.

What it solves

Patrols first, sweeps only where it is needed

/01
End-to-end embodied AI model

The machine runs the manufacturer’s proprietary end-to-end embodied AI model, integrating perception, prediction and action. Its generalisation capability allows it to handle new sites and tasks without scene-specific retraining.

/02
Patrol and sweep, on demand

The robot patrols and sweeps in a single pass, identifying debris on the road surface and engaging the cleaning system only where it is needed — eliminating wasted work on clean stretches.

/03
254 TOPS automotive-grade computing

High on-board computing power supports real-time inference of the end-to-end model, closing the perception–prediction–decision loop in mixed pedestrian and vehicle environments.

/04
1330 mm working width

A 1330 mm cleaning width including 500 mm side brushes delivers 3,000–4,500 m²/h, allowing a single machine to cover large outdoor hard-surface areas.

/05
AEB active collision avoidance

LiDAR and surround-view cameras are fused with an automatic emergency braking system to maintain safety in open environments shared with pedestrians.

/06
20 % gradeability

A maximum gradient of 20 % allows the machine to handle park ramps, basement entrances and undulating scenic paths. Working speed is infinitely variable from 0 to 5 km/h.

/07
5 m² filtration area

A filter system with 5 m² of filtration area, combined with a 100 W suction motor and an 800 W main brush motor, controls airborne dust escape during sweeping.

/08
IPX6 protection, long endurance

IPX6 ingress protection allows operation in rain. A 36 V / 125 Ah LiFePO₄ battery supports 6–8 hours of continuous work at a maximum machine power of only 1500 W.
