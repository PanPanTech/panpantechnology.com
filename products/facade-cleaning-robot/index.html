What it solves

Built around real site constraints

/01
Designed around facade workflow

Commercial facade cleaning depends on access, anchoring, surface geometry, water management and recovery procedures, so the page guides customers to share building information first.

/02
Reduces repetitive work-at-height exposure

On suitable repeated glass zones, the robot can move routine cleaning away from rope-only manual work and into a more controlled machine-assisted workflow.

/03
Works with hybrid cleaning plans

Robots handle regular glass areas while trained technicians remain responsible for setup, inspection, corners, signage, recesses and other difficult facade details.

/04
Supports multiple facade materials

Project assessment can cover pure flat glass, framed curtain walls, stone curtain walls, metal curtain walls and composite facade zones.

/05
Pilot before rollout

A representative pilot zone verifies cleaning quality, setup time, water supply, operating limits, recovery path and manual follow-up requirements.

/06
Procurement-ready information request

The page asks customers to submit facade drawings, photos, cleaning frequency, working hours and safety requirements through the website form.
