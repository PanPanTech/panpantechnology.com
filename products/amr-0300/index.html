AMR-0300 Industrial Transport Robot (300 kg class)

The AMR-0300 is an autonomous mobile robot platform for industrial logistics and heavy-duty commercial delivery, available in Standard, Lifting, Conveyor, Tray and Towing configurations. Built on a high-load chassis with an open architecture, it carries up to 300 kg (400 kg in the Towing version) and runs 12 hours unloaded. A 10.1-inch on-board touchscreen and IoT capabilities including elevator control, e-door access and call buttons allow it to integrate directly into existing production and warehouse systems.

What it solves

Built around real site constraints

/01
Five configurations on one platform

Standard, Lifting, Conveyor, Tray and Towing versions share the same chassis platform — specify by process need while keeping spare parts and training common across the fleet.

/02
300 kg payload with open architecture

A high-load chassis with open software and hardware interfaces simplifies system integration and hardware expansion; the Towing version pulls multiple carts up to 400 kg.

/03
12-hour runtime with 30 Ah swappable battery

Twelve hours unloaded and a 2-hour charge to 90%; charging station, wired charging and manual battery swap are all supported for multi-shift operation.

/04
±1 cm precise docking

Standard positioning accuracy is ±5 cm, tightening to ±1 cm in precise-connection mode — sufficient for automated pick-up and delivery at production lines.

/05
VSLAM + LiDAR SLAM fusion navigation

Vision and LiDAR SLAM are fused for positioning without QR codes or magnetic tape, with push-to-deploy commissioning and minimal site modification.

/06
Full IoT integration

Elevator control, e-door access, call buttons, dashboard, conveyor docking and Bluetooth speaker enable autonomous operation across floors and zones.

/07
Designed to ISO 3691-4

Engineered to the industrial mobile robot safety standard for use in factories and warehouses where people are present.
