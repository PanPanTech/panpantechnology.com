AFR-0400 Vacuum Wall-Climbing Robot for Industrial Facades

The AFR-0400 is a wall-climbing work robot for industrial facades. A dual-duct vacuum system holds it directly on concrete, steel, FRP and aluminium surfaces, so it needs no gondola, boom lift or full rope-and-winch system — only a single fall-arrest line fixed at the top. A quick-release mount takes an airless oscillating spray module, a high-pressure washing module or a grinding kit, and the operator works from the ground with a handheld controller and a live camera feed.

How it works

Vacuum, tools and a single safety line

The AFR-0400 is a work platform rather than a glass cleaner: it carries the tool to the wall and holds it there while the operator controls the job from the ground.

01

Dual-duct vacuum

Two vacuum ducts seal the robot against the surface. A one-way debris outlet stops grit and overspray from clogging the vacuum chamber.

02

Quick-release tool mount

A four-hole interface swaps between the spray module, high-pressure washing module and grinding kit without rigging changes.

03

Ground control with live view

A handheld controller with a 5.5" 1080p screen shows the front camera feed and sets travel, spray and sweep parameters.

04

Single fall-arrest line

One quick-lock safety line fixed at the top is the only rigging. Local work-at-height rules still apply and the line must be attached before operation.

AFR-0400 FAQ

Questions about industrial wall work

Can the AFR-0400 clean glass curtain walls?
It is built for industrial surfaces — concrete, steel, FRP and aluminium — and for jobs such as coating and pressure washing. For routine glass curtain-wall cleaning on office towers and hotels, use the AFR-1500 or AFR-1200.
Which surfaces can it hold on to?
Flat to moderately rough surfaces that allow a vacuum seal, from horizontal to vertical (0–90°). Deep corrugated or trapezoidal cladding with ribs above roughly 5 mm is not suitable, and it clears only small obstacles such as weld seams and bolt heads (about 5 mm).
Does it need a gondola or boom lift?
No. The dual-duct vacuum holds it on the wall, so only a single fall-arrest safety line fixed at the top is needed. The quick-lock line is attached or released in under 10 seconds.
What coatings can it spray, and how fast?
The oscillating airless spray module applies epoxy, polyurethane and other industrial coatings at 100–300 m²/h, depending on surface condition, coating and method. Spray on/off, sweep speed and angle are set from the controller.
How many people does it take?
It is a light, two-person setup: 23 kg robot, power, pump and operator on the ground. The operator controls it from up to 500 m away with live video from the front camera.
What maintenance does it need?
The vacuum skirt seals are the main wear part — a set of six lasts about 167 hours, and 1–2 spare sets should be kept on site. Sessions of about 60 minutes are recommended. The robot carries a 12-month warranty.
