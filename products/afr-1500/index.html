AFR-1500 High-Rise Facade Cleaning Robot

The AFR-1500 is a facade cleaning robot for high-rise and super-tall building exteriors, replacing conventional rope access, gondola and suspended-platform working methods. At only 50 kg, it offers an adjustable cleaning width of 600–1,500 mm and three cleaning modes rated up to 500 m²/h, with a maximum working height of 300 m, 220 mm obstacle clearance, Force 6 wind resistance and IP54 protection. It suits frameless flat glass, exposed-frame glass, stone, metal and composite facade systems.

What it solves

Taking people off the outside of the building

/01
Takes people off the building face

Conventional rope access, gondolas and suspended rails carry severe fall risk. Replacing manual climbing with a robot removes that exposure at source.

/02
Solves the shrinking, ageing rope-access workforce

High-rise cleaning crews are ageing with few replacements entering the trade. Robotic cleaning does not depend on a scarce high-risk occupation, making the service sustainable and repeatable.

/03
Up to 500 m²/h across three modes

Three cleaning modes — L, G and H — deliver 180, 360 and 500 m²/h respectively, selectable to match soil level and programme deadlines.

/04
Adjustable 600–1,500 mm cleaning width

Cleaning width adjusts between 600 and 1,500 mm to match facade panel modules, reducing repositioning passes.

/05
300 m working height, 220 mm obstacle clearance

A maximum working height of 300 m and 220 mm obstacle clearance let the robot pass transoms and decorative mullions on complex elevations.

/06
Force 6 wind resistance and IP54 protection

Rated to Force 6 wind with IP54 ingress protection for stable operation under normal outdoor working conditions.

/07
Suits multiple facade systems

Compatible with frameless flat glass, exposed vertical/horizontal frame glass, stone, metal and composite curtain wall systems.
