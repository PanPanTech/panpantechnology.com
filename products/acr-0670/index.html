ACR-0670 High-Suction Sweeping & Vacuum Robot

The ACR-0670 is a high-suction sweeping and vacuum robot developed specifically for the garment and textile industry. A 670 mm working width and 500–800 m²/h cleaning capacity are paired with a 16 L extra-large dust box, allowing roughly 4,000 m² to be cleaned on a single fill. Suction and brush design are optimised for fabric strips, cut pieces, thread, hair, fibres, fluff and cotton wadding — the debris conventional equipment struggles with.

What it solves

Built around real site constraints

/01
Suction engineered for textile debris

Suction power and brush geometry are optimised for fabric strips, cut pieces, thread, hair, fibres, fluff and cotton wadding — collected in a single pass where standard equipment clogs or leaves residue.

/02
16 L dust box — about 4,000 m² per fill

The extra-large dust box sharply reduces emptying frequency; combined with 4–6 hours of runtime, production lines and large warehouses can be covered in one deployment.

/03
670 mm working width at 500–800 m²/h

A wide working path and steady travel speed significantly increase the area covered per hour.

/04
4–6 hours of runtime

A 60 Ah battery supports 4–6 hours of continuous work and recharges in 3 hours, covering a full production shift.

/05
Compact docking station

A 425 × 220 × 533 mm station with 100–240 V wide-voltage input suits power supplies worldwide, with a small footprint and flexible placement.

/06
Quiet operation below 70 dB

Operating noise stays under 70 dB, so the robot can run during production hours without interfering with communication on the floor.

/07
8° gradeability and stable travel

0.8 m/s travel speed with 8° gradeability handles the ramps and threshold transitions common in workshops.
