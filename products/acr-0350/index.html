ACR-0350 Compact Full-Stack Cleaning Robot

The ACR-0350 is a compact cleaning robot built for small and mid-size indoor spaces and narrow aisles. At just 400 × 400 × 360 mm and 27 kg unloaded, it delivers full-stack cleaning — sweeping, vacuuming, mopping and scrubbing — in a single pass using a sweep-front, vacuum-centre, scrub-rear layout. It passes aisles as narrow as 60 cm, runs below 60 dB, and its actively extending side brush achieves genuine zero-edge cleaning.

What it solves

Built around real site constraints

/01
Sweep, vacuum and scrub in one pass

Dry and wet soil are handled simultaneously — three cleaning stages completed in a single pass, with no need to cover the same area twice.

/02
Passes 60 cm aisles

A 400 mm body width clears aisles down to 60 cm, reaching between racking, office partitions and corridors that conventional machines cannot enter.

/03
Actively extending side brush for true zero-edge cleaning

The side brush extends to work along the edge with a minimum wall distance of zero, leaving no residue at skirting boards and corners.

/04
Separate dry and wet channels prevent recontamination

Independent dry and wet paths with multi-stage antibacterial filtration keep fine particles contained and avoid secondary contamination.

/05
Twin fans with independent ducts, 15 kPa suction

A twin-fan design with separate air ducts delivers 15 kPa per channel for effective deep cleaning.

/06
AI debris recognition with spot cleaning

An intelligent debris recognition algorithm identifies soiled areas and cleans them directly, reducing unproductive travel.

/07
Quiet operation and modular servicing

Below 60 dB the robot can run during business hours; a modular component design keeps routine maintenance simple.
