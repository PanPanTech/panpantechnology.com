ASR-0012 Embodied AI Retail Service Robot

The ASR-0012 is a general-purpose embodied AI service robot for retail — not a machine that follows fixed routes, but one that understands spoken instructions, makes sense of shelves it has never seen and breaks tasks down to complete them itself. It has 12 degrees of freedom, a 720° blind-spot-free HD RGB-D camera, a 6-DoF arm rated at 3 kg and centimetre-level localisation. Zero-shot recognition lets it start work in a new store without site-specific training.

ASR-0012 FAQ

Questions retailers ask about embodied AI robots

What does “zero-shot recognition” mean for a store?
The robot recognises products, objects and shelf layouts it has never been trained on, using open-vocabulary vision models. A new store or a new SKU does not need a fresh round of photo collection and labelling before the robot can work there.
Which tasks can the ASR-0012 do today?
Three retail jobs: patrolling shelves to find out-of-stocks and merchandising errors, restocking shelves from totes, and picking and consolidating online or takeout orders.
What can the arm handle?
The 6-DoF arm is rated at 3 kg, which covers most packaged retail goods. It judges each item's material — glass, metal, wood — and adjusts grip force and approach to avoid crushing or dropping it.
Single-arm or dual-arm?
Single-arm suits picking and restocking of individual items. The dual-arm version is for tasks that need two hands, such as holding a tote while picking from it.
How long does deployment take?
Because recognition needs no site-specific training, the robot is designed for out-of-the-box deployment. Allow time for a store survey and a supervised pilot to confirm accuracy, restocking rate and exception handling in your stores.
Where are the dimensions, weight and runtime?
Overall dimensions, weight, runtime, charging, ingress protection, noise, passability, gradeability and operating temperature are confirmed on the factory technical data sheet, which we send with the quotation.
How is it different from the AHR-1636?
The ASR-0012 is purpose-built for retail stock checks, restocking and order picking with a 3 kg arm. The AHR-1636 is a general-purpose humanoid with two 10 kg arms for a broader range of service and light industrial tasks.
