ACR-0290 Compact Autonomous Floor Scrubber with Station

The ACR-0290 is a compact scrubbing robot for showrooms, offices, restaurants, hotel public areas and small to mid-size shops. At 429 × 480 × 283 mm and 22.3 kg it reaches under tables and into narrow spaces. Its all-in-one station refills clean water, drains dirty water, and washes and dries the robot automatically, while multi-camera vision handles mapping, navigation and obstacle avoidance — daily floor cleaning without manual handling.

What it solves

Compact enough for the spaces a full-size machine cannot reach

/01
Compact body

429 × 480 × 283 mm and 22.3 kg: gets under tables and into narrow aisles, and one person can carry it between areas.

/02
Fully automatic station

Refills and drains water automatically, then self-cleans and dries the robot — no daily tank handling.

/03
Multi-camera vision

One-key mapping, visual obstacle avoidance and autonomous navigation; a floor-material sensor detects the surface type.

/04
20 kPa suction, 290 mm roller

A 20 mm wide debris intake with 20 kPa suction handles wet and dry soil together, with near-zero edge cleaning.

/05
Five cleaning modes

Including detergent scrubbing and floor sanitising, at up to 0.8 m/s.

/06
Fast charging

2 h runtime and 1.5 h fast charge; returns to the station between tasks.
