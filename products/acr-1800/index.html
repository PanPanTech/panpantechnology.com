ACR-1800 Autonomous Outdoor Sweeping Robot

The ACR-1800 is a driverless outdoor sweeping robot for very large open areas. A variable 1,000–1,800 mm sweeping path delivers up to 10,000 m²/h at a cleaning rate of ≥99%, with a debris handling capacity of 60 L/min and automatic loading and unloading of 120 L municipal-standard bins. Scheduled deployment, obstacle avoidance and self-extraction, adaptive parameter tuning, automatic charging and water refilling, and autonomous return in rain all run without operator intervention.

What it solves

Built around real site constraints

/01
10,000 m²/h at ≥99% cleaning rate

A variable 1,000–1,800 mm sweeping path and 60 L/min debris capacity handle sand, dust, leaves, cans, peel, bottles, plastic bags, cigarette ends and paper waste with equal ease.

/02
Automatic handling of 120 L municipal bins

Built to municipal sanitation standards, sweeping, emptying and transfer are seamlessly connected — no manual waste handling required.

/03
All-weather unmanned operation

Scheduled departure, intelligent obstacle avoidance and self-extraction, adaptive operating parameters, fill-level detection, automatic charging and water refilling, and autonomous return in rain are all handled automatically.

/04
28 sensors with 150 m extended-range perception

Two LiDARs, eleven ultrasonic probes, four depth cameras, six colour cameras, an inertial measurement unit and contact sensors are fused for 360° real-time perception with millisecond safety response.

/05
Break-point resume — nothing missed, nothing repeated

After a pause for water, charging or bin replacement, the machine resumes cleaning from the exact break-point, avoiding both repeated passes and missed areas.

/06
Dust suppression prevents secondary contamination

A spray suppression system and automatic dust-shaking filter, with a 50 L tank supporting 90 minutes of continuous spraying, keep dust from being redistributed during sweeping.

/07
Vehicle-grade modular design

Modular construction allows disassembly and maintenance without specialist staff or tools, while built-in self-diagnostics provide status monitoring, wear prediction and fault alerts.
