ACR-0520 All-in-One Commercial Cleaning Robot

The ACR-0520 combines sweeping, scrubbing, vacuuming, dust-mopping, self-cleaning and sanitising in a single 6-in-1 machine. At just 650 × 580 × 550 mm and 70 kg, it switches between cleaning modes with one tap — no parts to change — delivering up to 1,197 m²/h of scrubbing with 4.5 hours of runtime. Built for offices, hotels, healthcare and small to mid-size commercial spaces.

What it solves

Built around real site constraints

/01
6-in-1: one machine replaces a fleet

Sweeping, scrubbing, vacuuming, dust-mopping, self-cleaning and sanitising in one unit. Switch modes from the on-board screen or the app — no modules to swap, a simpler equipment set to operate and maintain.

/02
5-second tool-free module release

Scrubbing brush, sweeping brush, dust box and mop pad all release single-handed in about 5 seconds. Daily upkeep needs no tools and no specialist training.

/03
HEPA H13 filtration

Captures 99.97% of fine particles — suitable for offices, healthcare and food-related areas, and prevents dust being recirculated.

/04
55 dB — quiet enough for opening hours

55 dB when scrubbing and 45 dB when dust-mopping, so the robot can work in guest and office areas during the day without disturbing people.

/05
Long-cycle LiFePO₄ battery

25.6 V / 40 Ah lithium iron phosphate pack rated for 2,000 charge cycles, with automatic return-to-dock at 10% and a full charge in under 4 hours.

/06
15 sensors, 360° fused perception

LiDAR, two depth cameras, five line lasers, six ultrasonic sensors and a bumper strip give 0.02–50 m coverage for autonomous mapping, path planning and obstacle avoidance.
