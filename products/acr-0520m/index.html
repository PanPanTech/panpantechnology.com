ACR-0520M Cleaning Robot with Advertising Display

The ACR-0520M adds a 27-inch Full HD advertising display to the ACR-0520 cleaning platform, for shopping centres, supermarkets, airports and exhibition halls with heavy foot traffic. While it scrubs, vacuums and dust-mops, the robot plays video and image content as it moves, extending reach and frequency. The screen detaches quickly and can also run while the robot is parked as a fixed display — turning a cleaning machine into a mobile media asset.
