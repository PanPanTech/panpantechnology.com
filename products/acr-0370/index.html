ACR-0370 4-in-1 Commercial Cleaning Robot

The ACR-0370 combines scrubbing, sweeping, vacuuming and dust-mopping in a single 4-in-1 commercial cleaning robot. A compact 580 × 500 × 600 mm body weighing 68.5 kg carries 21 sensors for full 360° protection, and is available with either 2D single-line LiDAR or 3D multi-line LiDAR navigation. The matching docking station handles waste discharge, water refilling and charging automatically — suited to workshops, offices, hotels, hospitals, metro stations and campuses.

What it solves

Built around real site constraints

/01
Four cleaning functions, switched in one tap

Scrubbing, sweeping, vacuuming and dust-mopping in a single machine, switched by mode to match the task — one unit covers the full daily floor-care routine.

/02
21 sensors with 360° protection

A multi-sensor fusion package derived from autonomous-driving technology delivers blind-spot-free perception for safe operation in occupied environments.

/03
Choice of 2D or 3D navigation

Available with 2D single-line LiDAR or 3D multi-line LiDAR, so the specification can be matched to site complexity and budget.

/04
Docking station: auto discharge, refill and charge

A 25 L clean / 25 L waste station performs water refilling, waste discharge and charging automatically, reducing manual intervention and supporting long unattended shifts.

/05
Three scrubbing module configurations

Roller brush + roller mop, twin roller brush or twin roller mop — selected to suit the floor material and soil type on site.

/06
4G, Wi-Fi or fully offline operation

Sites with restricted connectivity can run the robot offline; the app supports 19 languages for multi-region deployment and management.

/07
Compact body for varied indoor floors

A 580 mm body width passes standard aisles; suitable for marble, terrazzo, ceramic tile, PVC, epoxy, engineered stone and short-pile carpet.
