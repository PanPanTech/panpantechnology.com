ACR-0440C Commercial Cleaning Robot

The ACR-0440C is a commercial cleaning robot for indoor environments. A 503 × 503 × 629 mm body weighing 42 kg delivers a 440 mm cleaning width at up to 800 m²/h, supported by separate 10 L clean and 10 L recovery tanks. Navigation combines 2D LiDAR (3D optional) with obstacle radar, ultrasonic sensors and an HD camera, while a 1 kWh lithium iron phosphate battery supports extended continuous operation.

What it solves

Built around real site constraints

/01
800 m²/h of cleaning capacity

A 440 mm cleaning width combined with steady travel speed delivers up to 800 m²/h, covering medium and large commercial areas in a single run.

/02
Scrubbing, mopping and vacuuming modes

Switch between scrubbing, mopping and vacuuming (optional) to match floor type and soil conditions.

/03
10 L + 10 L twin tanks

Separate 10 L clean and 10 L recovery tanks reduce mid-shift refilling and draining, extending time on task.

/04
Multi-sensor fusion navigation

2D LiDAR (3D optional) works with obstacle radar, ultrasonic sensors, anti-drop sensors and an HD camera for autonomous avoidance in busy environments.

/05
1 kWh lithium iron phosphate battery

LiFePO₄ cells provide long cycle life and high safety, with 1 kWh of capacity for extended continuous operation.

/06
4G / 5G / Wi-Fi connectivity

Multiple connectivity options suit varying site conditions and support remote management and fleet scheduling.

/07
Modular quick-release maintenance

Tanks, brushes and battery release quickly, so routine servicing and replacement need no specialist tools.
