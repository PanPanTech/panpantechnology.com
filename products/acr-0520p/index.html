ACR-0520P Fully Autonomous Floor Scrubber with Working Station

The ACR-0520P is the top configuration of the ACR-0520 platform, built for shopping centres, office towers, factories and transport hubs that need genuinely unattended 24/7 cleaning. Its working station connects directly to the water supply and drain: the robot refills and empties itself during work, the station washes the roller brush, and detergent is dosed automatically. A 3D LiDAR and a wide-angle depth camera provide dual perception, while AI vision recognises 20+ object and stain classes and adjusts the cleaning strategy on its own.

What it solves

A station that removes the manual steps around the clean

/01
Truly unattended

The station is plumbed to water supply and drain for automatic refilling and emptying, washes the roller brush itself and doses detergent from a built-in reservoir.

/02
Safe in complex spaces

Industrial 3D LiDAR plus a wide-angle depth camera build a centimetre-level model of the environment, predict moving objects and re-plan in milliseconds.

/03
AI recognition

Identifies 20+ targets such as paper, sauce spills and table legs for millimetre-level avoidance; switches to intensive mode on stubborn stains; an auxiliary light keeps vision working in low light.

/04
Dry behind the robot

A quick-release squeegee and high-power vortex suction fan reach 93 % water recovery, reducing residual water and slip risk. Squeegee blades change by hand.

/05
Three power modes

Fast charging in about 1.5 h, modular battery swap, or conventional direct charging — top up between tasks for high-frequency operation.

/06
Ergonomic push handle

Move the robot between areas, spot-clean or map a new site by hand with one-handed control and no specialist training.
