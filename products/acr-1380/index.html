ACR-1380 Autonomous Outdoor Sweeper

The ACR-1380 is a fully autonomous outdoor sweeper for commercial plazas, business parks, residential communities and scenic areas. Built on an automotive-grade painted steel body, it combines main-brush sweeping, high-power vacuuming, directional air blowing and mist dust suppression in one four-in-one system. A 1380 mm working width and a 150 L hopper allow 6–8 hours of unattended operation, and with automatic recharging and water refilling the machine and its dock form a complete unattended cycle.

What it solves

Four cleaning actions in one unattended cycle

/01
Four-in-one cleaning

Main-brush sweeping, vacuuming, directional blowing and mist dust suppression work together to handle grit, fine dust and leaves while suppressing airborne dust.

/02
5 cm edge cleaning

Precision sensing and edge-following control give centimetre-level edge sweeping down to a 5 cm offset, clearing kerbs and corners conventional machines leave behind.

/03
150 m long-range sensing

LiDAR, vision and ultrasonic sensors are fused into an all-round perception network. Obstacles are detected up to 150 m ahead, and low obstacles from 10 cm are reliably identified.

/04
Resume-from-interruption

Full-coverage path planning records progress and position when work is paused; after recharging or emptying, the machine resumes from the exact point of interruption.

/05
10-minute deployment

Mapping is done remotely or on site through a simple interface requiring no surveying expertise. A new site is typically commissioned in about 10 minutes.

/06
Auto-recharge and auto-refill

When battery or water runs low the machine returns to the dock on its own; scheduled tasks and automatic return enable unattended operation.

/07
All-terrain capability

Operates reliably on plaza pavers, concrete, asphalt, rubber running tracks, marble and permeable brick.

/08
App and cloud management

Mapping and task planning, cleaning reports, real-time status monitoring, multi-user permissions, remote OTA updates and fault pre-warning.
